Logistics Management|Final Mile

Home & Business Delivery

Flexible solutions with your customers’ needs in mind.

The way you deliver your product is critical to your customer’s impression of your brand. Your customers expect precision, quality and a great experience from start-to-finish. Whether you’re delivering to a customer’s residence, business park or retail store, having the flexibility and control over the customer’s experience is an important part of ensuring your brand’s quality is kept intact throughout the entire experience.

Hub Group’s final mile solutions put you in full control over your last mile operations, providing access to a robust carrier network operating out of over 200 nationwide terminals and advanced analytical tools that improve decision making.

Our team hand-selects, optimizes and executes carriers that match your business requirements, all while providing a seamless experience to your teams and customers.

For final mile, visibility is essential to manage customer expectations. We employ state-of-the-art tracking tools to provide a real-time perspective into shipments throughout your network, helping to manage exceptions and adjust service on the fly – keeping your customers’ expectations aligned.

A Personalized Final Mile Logistics Solution

We operate as an extension of your brand, ensuring your customers’ satisfaction.

Basic or Threshold Service

Delivery to the first available dry area or across the threshold with a hand-selected provider based on your unique freight requirements.

Room of Choice Service

Delivery to a specific part of your customer’s residence or place of business with a flexible delivery schedule.

White Glove Services

Add the final touches to your customers’ experience with on-site delivery, setup, deluxing, appliance installation, removals and more.

Appliance Delivery, Installation and Removal

Deliver precision, expertise and professionalism to your customers with on-site setup and haul away.
