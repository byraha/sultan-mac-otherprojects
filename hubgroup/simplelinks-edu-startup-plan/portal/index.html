📊 The Research

The Academia-Industry Gap in Bangalore

After analyzing 200+ Naukri job listings and the complete VTU 2022 CSE syllabus, here's what we found.

🎓

Theory Overload, Practice Starvation

VTU covers OS, DBMS, Networks, and ML — but students learn theory in isolation. They can implement a BST but can't debug a slow database query or explain how Docker uses cgroups.

💼

42.6% Employability

Only 42.6% of Indian engineering graduates are employable (Mercer-Mettl 2025). 95% are unfit for software development roles. Colleges spend ₹5-15L/year on external training with mixed results.

📋

Curriculum vs. Industry Reality

Students learn SQL but not query optimization. They study OS but not production debugging. They learn ML algorithms but never deploy a model. The theory is there — the application layer is missing.

🔗

Willingness to Pay is High

Colleges are desperate for industry engagement. Low placement % hurts enrollment and accreditation. HODs actively seek partners who can bridge this gap — and they have budget allocated for it.
