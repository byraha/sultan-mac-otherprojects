# 07 — Competitive Analysis

## 7.1 Direct Competitors

### Coding Ninjas
- **Focus:** DSA, competitive programming, full-stack development
- **Format:** Online + offline centers
- **Pricing:** ₹15,000–50,000 per course
- **Strengths:** Brand recognition, structured curriculum, large team
- **Weaknesses:** Generic — not aligned to any university curriculum. Expensive for many students. No college partnership model.
- **Threat Level:** Medium (indirect — students compare)
- **SimpleLinks Advantage:** Curriculum-aligned, cheaper, college-embedded

### UpGrad / Great Learning
- **Focus:** Full-stack, data science, AI/ML
- **Format:** Online, 6–12 month programs
- **Pricing:** ₹50,000–3,00,000
- **Strengths:** Strong brand, placement assistance, global reach
- **Weaknesses:** Very expensive, not targeted at current engineering students, no on-campus presence
- **Threat Level:** Low (different market segment)

### ICT Academy
- **Focus:** Faculty development, student skilling
- **Format:** Government-funded workshops
- **Pricing:** Free or subsidized
- **Strengths:** Government backing, wide reach, low cost to colleges
- **Weaknesses:** Bureaucratic, slow, inconsistent quality, batch-based (not continuous)
- **Threat Level:** Medium (colleges may prefer free option)
- **SimpleLinks Advantage:** Quality, speed, customization, industry relevance

### Local Training Partners (Mom-and-Pop)
- **Focus:** Placement training, soft skills, aptitude
- **Format:** On-campus, batch-based
- **Pricing:** ₹3–8L per batch
- **Strengths:** Local relationships, low cost
- **Weaknesses:** Inconsistent quality, outdated content, no technical depth
- **Threat Level:** High (they have relationships)
- **SimpleLinks Advantage:** Technical depth, industry currency, modern content

## 7.2 Indirect Competitors

| Player | What They Do | Why They're Not Direct Competition |
|--------|-------------|-----------------------------------|
| **YouTube / free content** | Free CS tutorials | No structure, no accountability, no college integration |
| **College professors** | Deliver curriculum | Don't have industry experience, stick to textbook |
| **Coursera / edX** | Online degrees, specializations | Expensive, not integrated with college schedule |
| **LinkedIn Learning** | Short video courses | Too generic, no hands-on projects |
| **LeetCode / HackerRank** | Coding practice | Only DSA, no holistic CS education |

## 7.3 Competitive Moats for SimpleLinks Edu

1. **Curriculum Integration** — We align with what students are *currently* studying. Not a separate thing they do on the side.

2. **Industry Credibility** — You have real industry experience (Windows Senior Admin, MSP background). Most trainers don't.

3. **On-Campus Presence** — Physical presence at colleges builds trust and accountability that pure online players can't match.

4. **Relationship Network** — Once you have 5 college relationships, switching costs increase (logistics, trust, student familiarity).

5. **Feedback Loop** — Direct interaction with students every semester → continuous content improvement.

6. **Cost Advantage** — Lean operation (you + interns) means you can charge less than competitors while delivering more value.

## 7.5 Key Insight

The biggest competitor is actually **the status quo** — colleges doing nothing because it's easier. The sale is not just about your offering; it's about convincing colleges that the cost of *not* acting is higher than the cost of partnering with you.

**Your strongest argument:** "42.6% employability means 57.4% of your students are paying ₹3–5L for a degree that won't get them a job. We can change that."
