# 03 — Business Model & Pricing

## 3.1 Revenue Streams

| Stream | Description | Target Customer | Gross Margin |
|--------|-------------|-----------------|-------------|
| **College Partnership (Annual)** | 2–4 modules per semester across multiple subjects | Engineering colleges | 60–70% |
| **Student Bootcamps (Direct)** | Weekend/intensive courses sold directly to students | Students (B2C) | 80–85% |
| **Faculty Development** | Train-the-trainer programs | Colleges | 70–80% |
| **Online Subscription** | Recorded courses with live support | Students nationwide | 85–90% |
| **Placement Preparation** | Mock interviews, company-specific prep | Students (B2C) / Colleges (B2B) | 75–85% |
| **Corporate Training** | Fresher onboarding programs for IT companies | Companies | 70–80% |

## 3.2 Pricing Strategy

### Tier A: College Partnership (B2B)

| Package | Modules Included | Price (per semester) | Annual Value |
|---------|-----------------|---------------------|--------------|
| **Basic** | 1 subject module (8 hrs) + 1 guest lecture | ₹1,50,000 | ₹3,00,000 |
| **Standard** | 2 subject modules + 1 bridge course (16 hrs total) + placement prep | ₹3,00,000 | ₹6,00,000 |
| **Premium** | 4 subject modules + 2 bridge courses + faculty training + placement support | ₹5,00,000 | ₹10,00,000 |

Pricing rationale: ₹3–5L per semester = ~₹15–25K per student per year (for a class of 60). Colleges spend ₹5–15L annually on external training partners, often with lower quality.

### Tier B: Direct to Student (B2C)

| Product | Format | Price | Target |
|---------|--------|-------|--------|

### Tier C: Online Subscription

## 3.4 Revenue Projection (Year 1)

| Quarter | College Partners | Student Bootcamps | Online | Revenue |
|---------|----------------|-------------------|--------|---------|
| Q1 (Months 1–3) | 1 pilot (₹1.5L) | 2 bootcamps × 30 students × ₹3K = ₹1.8L | Pre-launch | ₹3.3L |
| Q2 (Months 4–6) | 2 partners (₹4.5L) | 3 bootcamps × 40 students × ₹3.5K = ₹4.2L | ₹0.5L | ₹9.2L |
| Q3 (Months 7–9) | 3 partners (₹7.5L) | 3 bootcamps × 50 students × ₹4K = ₹6L | ₹1L | ₹14.5L |
| Q4 (Months 10–12) | 5 partners (₹12L) | 4 bootcamps × 60 students × ₹4K = ₹9.6L | ₹2L | ₹23.6L |
| **Total Y1** | **5 partners** | **12 bootcamps** | **Growing** | **~₹50L** |

**Conservative Estimate:** ₹18–25L in Year 1 (assuming slower sales cycle).
**Realistic Target:** ₹35–50L in Year 1.
**Stretch Goal:** ₹60L+ in Year 1.

## 3.5 Break-Even Analysis

- Monthly fixed costs: ~₹25,000 (averaged across year)
- Average revenue per college partnership: ₹3L/semester
- Break-even: **3 college partnerships** (~₹9L revenue) covers full year costs
- Timeline to break-even: **Month 6–8** (when 2–3 colleges are onboarded)

## 3.6 Pricing Principles

1. **Value-based, not cost-plus** — Price based on improved placement outcomes, not hours taught
2. **Low-risk entry** — Free guest lecture as a demo, paid only if they see value
3. **Tiered pricing** — Let colleges choose what they need and grow into higher tiers
4. **Student-friendly B2C** — ₹3–5K is affordable for most engineering students
5. **Early-adopter discount** — First 3 colleges get 20% off for Year 1
