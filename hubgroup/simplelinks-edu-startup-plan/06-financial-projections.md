## 6.4 Break-Even Analysis

- **Fixed costs (monthly):** ~₹22,500 (pre-revenue phase)
- **Blended gross margin:** ~85%
- **Monthly revenue needed to break even:** ₹22,500 / 0.85 = ~₹26,500
- **Break-even point:** Month 3 (when first college pilot + bootcamp revenue hits)

## 6.6 Funding Strategy

**Year 1:** Bootstrap (zero external funding). Revenue from first pilot funds growth.

**Year 2 (if pursuing):**
- Raise ₹20–30L angel round for expanding team and building tech platform
- Or continue bootstrapping (slower growth, higher margins)

**Year 3+:** 
- Series A target: ₹2–3Cr for national expansion
- Alternative: Stay profitable and grow organically

## 6.7 Key Financial Assumptions

| Assumption | Basis |
|------------|-------|
| College sales cycle: 4–6 weeks | Based on industry norms for educational services |
| B2B payment terms: 50% advance + 50% on completion | Negotiable, but advance payment protects cash flow |
| B2C conversion rate: 5–10% of engaged leads | Typical for ed-tech |
| Student retention in bootcamps: 70% | Based on comparable programs |
| College renewal rate: 80% | Assuming quality delivery and visible impact |
| Average revenue per college (Year 1): ₹3L/semester | Blended across Basic/Standard tiers |
