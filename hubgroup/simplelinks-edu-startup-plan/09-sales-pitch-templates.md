# SimpleLinks Edu — 90-Second Sales Pitch (for HODs/Principals)

## The Hook
> "Your 3rd-year CS students can implement a BST but can't debug a slow database query. I fix that."

## The Problem (20 sec)
- 57% of engineering graduates are unemployable
- VTU curriculum is theory-heavy
- Your students are competing with candidates who have practical experience
- College placement % is directly tied to practical skills

## The Solution (30 sec)
SimpleLinks Edu delivers **industry-integrated modules** that run alongside your existing curriculum. Not a replacement — a practical layer on top.

- Your professor teaches the theory
- I teach what it means in the real world
- Students build real projects, use real tools, solve real problems

## Proof (15 sec)
- [X] years of hands-on industry experience
- Currently working with [pilot college — name if exists]
- Students go from confused about theory to confident in interviews

## The Offer (15 sec)
- **Free 90-minute guest lecture** — no strings, just value
- Your students decide if it's useful
- If they love it, we discuss a pilot

## Close (10 sec)
"When can I come in for that free session?"
