## 8.7 If Things Go Wrong — Contingency Plans

| Scenario | Trigger | Action |
|----------|---------|--------|
| **No college signs up in 3 months** | 0 pilots | Pivot to B2C-only model, build brand through YouTube, target students directly |
| **Only 1–2 colleges, not progressing** | Slow growth | Lower price, offer free semester to build case studies |
| **Students don't attend sessions** | <50% attendance | Work with college to make sessions mandatory/credit-linked |
| **You get a full-time job offer** | Opportunity cost | Run SimpleLinks evenings/weekends, hire a full-time instructor |
| **Competitor copies your model** | Imitation | Double down on quality, relationships, and brand — competitors can copy content but not trust |
