# SimpleLinks Edu - Comprehensive Business Plan

## 1. COMPANY OVERVIEW

**SimpleLinks Edu** - Bridging the gap between academia and industry for engineering colleges in Bangalore.

**Mission**: To transform engineering education in Bangalore by delivering industry-relevant, practice-oriented learning experiences that make graduates job-ready from day one.

**Vision**: Become the most trusted education partner for every engineering college in Karnataka within 5 years.

**Core Values**:
- Practical over Theoretical
- Quality at Scale
- Industry-First Approach
- Student Success as the Only Metric

## 2. TARGET SEGMENTS

### Primary: Engineering Colleges in Bangalore (VTU Affiliated)
| Tier | Examples | Count (approx) | Characteristics |
|------|----------|----------------|-----------------|
| Tier 1 | RVCE, BMSCE, PES, MSRIT, Dayananda Sagar | ~10 | High fees, decent placements, want premium add-ons |
| Tier 2 | New Horizon, CMRIT, ACS, BCET, HKBK | ~40 | Moderate fees, moderate placements, NEED our services most |
| Tier 3 | Smaller/Newer colleges | ~50 | Low placements, desperate for improvement |

**Primary Focus**: Tier 2 colleges - they have budget, need improvement, open to partnerships.

### Secondary: Students (B2C)
- Direct-to-student workshops, weekend batches, interview prep
- Online courses and certifications
- Project guidance and mentorship

### Tertiary: Faculty
- Faculty Development Programs (FDPs)
- Research methodology workshops
- Industry exposure programs

## 3. SERVICE DELIVERY MODELS

### Model A: College Partnership (B2B - Primary Revenue)
**How it works**: Annual or semester-based MoU with college
- **Basic Tier**: ₹2-5 L/year - Workshop series (4 workshops/semester)
- **Standard Tier**: ₹5-10 L/year - Full lab delivery + faculty training + placement prep
- **Premium Tier**: ₹10-20 L/year - End-to-end CSE program support including curriculum design, lab setup, faculty training, placement guarantee program

### Model B: Direct-to-Student (B2C - Scalable)
- Weekend bootcamps: ₹5,000-15,000 per student
- Online courses: ₹2,000-8,000 per course
- 1:1 Mentorship: ₹500-1,000/hour
- Project guidance: ₹10,000-25,000 per project

### Model C: Faculty Training (B2B - High Margin)
- FDP workshops: ₹50,000-2,00,000 per program
- Certificate programs for faculty
- Research collaboration support

## 4. UNIQUE SELLING PROPOSITIONS

1. **Industry Practitioner Faculty** - Not academicians, but people who have built real products
2. **Live Project Based Learning** - Every concept taught via building something real
3. **Placement-Linked Outcomes** - We don't just teach; we ensure students get jobs
4. **VTU Syllabus Mapped** - Every workshop is mapped to the exact semester subjects
5. **Scalable Hybrid Model** - Online + offline blended delivery
6. **Continuous Learning Ecosystem** - LMS access, recorded sessions, community support

## 8. KEY RISKS & MITIGATION

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|------------|
| Colleges have low budgets | High | High | Start with low-cost workshops; prove value first |
| Faculty resistance to external partners | Medium | Medium | Involve faculty in co-creation; make them look good |
| Students don't attend | Medium | High | Make sessions mandatory via college; gamify learning |
| Quality dilution at scale | Medium | High | Create standard operating procedures; train-trainer model |
| Competition from other players | Low | Medium | Focus on hyper-local Bangalore; deep partnerships not possible for giants |
