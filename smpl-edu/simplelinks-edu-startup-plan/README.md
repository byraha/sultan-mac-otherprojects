### Quick Snapshot

**The Problem**: 83% of engineering graduates remain unplaced. Curriculum is theory-heavy. Faculty lacks industry experience. Colleges want to improve placements but don't know how.

**The Solution**: SimpleLinks Edu partners with colleges to deliver industry-led, practice-oriented workshops mapped to the VTU syllabus — making students job-ready while they study.

**The Market**: Bangalore has 100+ engineering colleges with ~60,000 CSE students. The India EdTech market is $7.5B and growing at 28% CAGR. Almost NO competitor is doing B2B college partnerships in Bangalore.

**Revenue Model**: Workshop fees (₹1-20L/year per college) + Direct-to-student programs + Faculty training.

**Year 1 Target**: 5 colleges, ₹40L revenue, profitable by month 4.
