# SimpleLinks Edu - Go-To-Market Strategy

## PHASE 1: VALIDATION (Months 1-3)

### Objective
Prove demand and deliver first paid workshops with minimal investment.

### Actions
1. **Identify 10 Target Colleges** (Tier 2 - moderate placement, need improvement)
   - ACS College of Engineering
   - Bangalore College of Engineering & Technology (BCET)
   - HKBK College of Engineering
   - CMR Institute of Technology
   - New Horizon College of Engineering
   - KS Institute of Technology
   - East Point College of Engineering
   - AMC Engineering College
   - Sambhram Institute of Technology
   - Dr. Ambedkar Institute of Technology

2. **Create Sample Workshop Content**
   - Record a 15-min demo video of one workshop
   - Build a sample project that students build in 2 days
   - Create one-pager brochure

3. **Outreach to Training & Placement Officers (TPOs)**
   - Email: Professional intro + one-pager
   - Follow-up call within 3 days
   - Offer: "FREE 1-day pilot workshop for your students. No commitment."

4. **Deliver 3-5 Free Pilot Workshops**
   - Collect feedback forms
   - Get testimonial videos
   - Track attendance and engagement
   - Convert to paid engagement

5. **Build Initial LMS** (simple MVP)
   - Use Teachable / Thinkific / Moodle (₹0-5K/month)
   - Upload workshop recordings, assignments
   - Start Telegram/WhatsApp community

### Success Metrics (Month 3)
- [ ] 3 paid college partnerships secured
- [ ] 10+ student testimonials
- [ ] ₹5 Lakh revenue
- [ ] 500 students reached through workshops

## PHASE 2: ESTABLISHMENT (Months 4-9)

### Objective
Build reputation, standardize delivery, expand partnerships.

### Actions
1. **Develop Full Workshop Catalog** (all Sem 3-6 subjects)
2. **Hire First Instructor** (industry-experienced, part-time initially)
3. **Launch First Faculty Development Program**
4. **Host 1 Free Webinar** per month on "Industry Trends for Engineering Students"
5. **Build College Sales Pipeline** (target: meet 1 new college TPO every week)
6. **Launch Placement Preparation Program** as standalone B2C offering
7. **Create SimpleLinks Edu YouTube Channel** - free educational content (lead generation)

### Success Metrics (Month 9)
- [ ] 10 college partnerships
- [ ] ₹30 Lakh revenue (run-rate)
- [ ] 2,000+ students engaged
- [ ] Repeat rate > 60%
- [ ] Net Promoter Score > 40

## PHASE 3: SCALE (Months 10-18)

### Objective
Scale across Bangalore, add online component, build team.

### Actions
1. **Tier 1 College Outreach** - RVCE, BMSCE, PES (premium workshops)
2. **Launch Online-Only Courses** for non-Bangalore students
3. **Build Custom LMS** (if validated by demand)
4. **Create Train-the-Trainer Program** to scale instructor capacity
5. **Apply for AICTE/NSDC/IKS Recognized Training Partner status**
6. **Expand to Nearby Cities** - Mysore, Hubli, Mangalore
7. **Offer Summer Internship Program** at scale

### Success Metrics (Month 18)
- [ ] 25+ college partnerships
- [ ] ₹2+ Cr annual revenue
- [ ] 8-10 team members
- [ ] Profitable operations

## SALES APPROACH FOR COLLEGE MEETINGS

### Pitch Structure (30 min meeting)

**Intro (2 min)**
"Hi, I'm [Name], founder of SimpleLinks Edu. We help engineering colleges make their students job-ready through industry-led workshops mapped to the VTU syllabus."

**Problem (3 min)**
- 83% engineers unplaced
- Faculty lacks industry experience
- VTU syllabus has modern subjects but colleges can't deliver them effectively
- Students don't know how to apply theory

**Solution (5 min)**
- We deliver 2-3 day workshops where students build real projects
- Workshops are mapped to their current semester subjects
- Industry practitioners (not academicians) conduct them
- Example: For ML subject, students build and deploy an ML model in 3 days

**Proof (5 min)**
- Show pilot testimonials
- Show student projects built in workshops
- Show placement improvement numbers

**Offer (5 min)**
- Start with 2 workshops this semester (₹1-3 L)
- Faculty can attend free
- First workshop at 50% discount for new partners

**Close (5 min)**
- "Can we do a free 1-day pilot for your 5th-sem students next month?"
- "Who else should we involve in this decision?"

## PRICING STRATEGY

### Penetration Pricing (Year 1)
- Underprice competitors by 30-40%
- Focus on volume and relationship building
- Free pilot workshops to build trust

### Value Pricing (Year 2)
- Once proven, increase prices 20-30%
- Bundle multiple services (workshop + LMS + placement)
- Premium tier for top colleges

### Outcome-Based Pricing (Optional)
- "Pay per placement" model
- Higher risk but very attractive to colleges
- ₹10,000 per student placed (on top of base fee)
