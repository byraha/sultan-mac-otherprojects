# SimpleLinks Edu - Detailed Service Offerings & Delivery Blueprint

## SERVICE 1: VTU SYLLABUS-MAPPED INDUSTRY WORKSHOPS

### Concept
For every core CSE subject in the VTU 2022 scheme, we offer a 2-4 day intensive workshop that takes what students learn in theory and shows them how it works in the real world.

### Delivery Format
- **Duration**: 2-4 consecutive days (16-32 hours total)
- **Format**: 40% lecture/demo, 60% hands-on coding/building
- **Batch Size**: 30-60 students per batch
- **Location**: College lab or SimpleLinks Edu hub
- **Includes**: Workshop kit (materials, VM images, code repos), certificate, LMS access for 6 months

## DELIVERY QUALITY STANDARDS

Every SimpleLinks Edu program follows these principles:

1. **Industry Practitioner as Instructor** - No academicians without industry experience
2. **Project-First Pedagogy** - Start with a goal, learn concepts as needed to achieve it
3. **Real Code, No Toy Examples** - Use production-grade code, modern frameworks
4. **GitHub-Based Submission** - All work submitted via pull requests
5. **Certificate of Completion** - With project details and skills validated
6. **Post-Program Support** - 1 month of doubt-clearing on WhatsApp/Telegram
7. **Feedback-Driven Iteration** - Every program improved based on student feedback
