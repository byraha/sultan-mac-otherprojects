## 5. BREAK-EVEN ANALYSIS

- **Total investment needed**: ₹5 Lakh (first 3 months)
- **Monthly fixed costs**: ₹2.5 L (from month 4)
- **Revenue per workshop**: ₹55,000 gross profit (avg)
- **Break-even workshops per month**: 5 workshops
- **Time to break-even**: Month 4 (assuming 5+ workshops/month)

## 6. FUNDING REQUIREMENT

| Phase | Amount Needed | Use of Funds | Timeline |
|-------|--------------|--------------|----------|
| Bootstrap (Months 1-3) | ₹5 Lakh | Setup, pilots, equipment | Immediate |
| Growth (Months 4-9) | ₹10 Lakh | Team expansion, marketing, 10 college reach | Month 4 |
| Scale (Months 10-18) | ₹25 Lakh | LMS development, city expansion, 30 colleges | Month 10 |

**Recommendation**: Bootstrap first 6 months. Prove model with 5-10 colleges. Then seek angel funding for scale.

## 7. KEY FINANCIAL METRICS TO TRACK

| Metric | Target (Year 1) | Why |
|--------|----------------|-----|
| **Revenue per college** | ₹8-10 L/year | Measures partnership depth |
| **Student acquisition cost** | < ₹200 | Through college channel is low-cost |
| **Workshop margin** | > 65% | Healthy service business |
| **College retention rate** | > 70% | Indicates value delivery |
| **Placement improvement** | > 15% | Our true north metric |
| **Net Promoter Score** | > 40 | Student satisfaction |
